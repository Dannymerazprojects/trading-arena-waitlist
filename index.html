The Arena

Built for Those Who Want More Control.

Trading Arena is a funded trading platform built around clear choices. Traders choose the rules, pricing, and payout path that fits how they trade, then work through a simple path from evaluation to payout.

I I

Choose

Select your account rules, account size, and pricing structure before you start.

II II

Prove

III III

Get Paid

After passing, choose your payout path and earn standard payouts, with the opportunity to compete for additional rewards.

The Platform

Designed to Give You More Flexibility.

Trading Arena is being built to give traders a clearer way to choose how they want to trade. Instead of one standard account structure, traders can choose the rules, pricing, and payout path that best fits their style.

01

Choose Your Rules

Select the account structure that fits how you trade, from lower-cost options with tighter rules to higher-flexibility options with easier account geometry.

02

Pick Your Price

Choose the account size and pricing structure that makes sense for you. Trading Arena is designed around one-time pricing, with no monthly fees and no activation fee.

03

Control Your Payout Path

After passing the evaluation, traders can choose the payout path that fits their goals, whether they want faster payout access or larger payout potential.

04

Earn Beyond Standard Payouts

Funded traders can earn standard payouts and compete for additional rewards through Trading Arena leaderboards and performance-based opportunities.
